# ChronoDino-Android-Game

ChronoDino is an Android-based virtual pet survival game where players must keep a dinosaur alive by feeding it before its life timer reaches zero. The dinosaur starts with 60 seconds of life, and the timer continuously decreases over time. Players must feed the dinosaur regularly to restore its health and earn points.

As the timer enters the final 10 seconds, the dinosaur becomes weak, displays hunger warnings, cries for food, and indicates that it is starving. If the timer reaches 0 seconds, the dinosaur dies and the game ends.

Players can feed the dinosaur using three different food items.

Each food item awards a specific number of points and restores the dinosaur's timer. To make the gameplay more engaging, the dinosaur occasionally develops food cravings. If the player provides the exact food that the dinosaur is craving, they receive 2× bonus points compared to the normal score of that food item.

The game also includes a High Score System, allowing players to track and beat their best scores across multiple gameplay sessions.

# Gameplay
**Start the game with a dinosaur that has 60 seconds of life.**
**Feed the dinosaur using Apple, Chicken, or Fruit.**
**Restore the timer and earn points for each feeding.**
**Watch for special food cravings.**
**Feed the correct craving item to earn 2× bonus points.**
**Prevent the timer from reaching zero.**
**Try to achieve the highest score possible and beat your previous record.**
